Zero-Trust Evidence Verifier

Independently repeats every operation it can: fetches the manifest, recomputes its RFC 8785 digest, checks its secp256k1 signature, and optionally reads the on-chain record. It never trusts a supplied hash, signature, CID, job ID, or "verified" flag — see Limitations below for exactly what is and isn't established.

Verification Report

What this does and does not establish:
  • Integrity: the retrieved bytes match the declared digest. Authenticity: the declared key produced a valid signature over that digest.
  • It does NOT recompute the IPFS CID's multihash from the retrieved bytes — content came from whatever gateway answered the request. See the "IPFS retrieval" line below each run.
  • A manifest's embedded public key proves cryptographic self-consistency, not that the key belongs to a specific person or organization — that needs a separate trust mechanism (DID/VC, certificate, registry) this page does not evaluate.
  • An on-chain record proves a hash/CID/proofType were anchored by a specific wallet at a specific time — it does not by itself establish the truth of any domain claim (legal, scientific, business, AI-visibility) the payload makes.
  • A DEMO-flagged record is never reported as VERIFIED, however cleanly its cryptography checks out.
